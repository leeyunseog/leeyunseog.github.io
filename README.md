# EDNEL homepage

Energy Device and Nano-Engineering Laboratory, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Seoul National University.

- The whole site is one file, `index.html`. Members, alumni, papers and research areas are lists near
  the top of its `<script>` (search for `const GROUPS`, `const ALUMNI`, `const PAPERS`, `const AREAS`).
- Portraits go in `img/people/` as 288x360 WebP, named in `PORTRAITS`.
- Visitor statistics: Google Analytics 4 (`const STATS`). Visits are counted only on the hosts listed there.
- `CNAME` holds the custom domain (energy.snu.ac.kr). Keep it in the repository root.
- `img/og.jpg` is the preview image shown when the link is shared.
